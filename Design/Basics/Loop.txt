The Loop:

This is a 2D side-scrolling survival horror game with stealth elements.
Players will find themselves aboard a large spacecraft with most of the crew turned into grotesque monsters.
Players are to navigate the ship, finding and using key items in their limited inventory space to unlock new parts of the ship.
It will be up to the player to either sneak past or fight their way through the ship to find answers and escape.

Major inspirations include, but are not limited to:
The Resident Evil Franchise
Signalis
Dead Space
Prey
Metal Gear Solid
Splinter Cell (specifically the games on GBA, as they are side-scrollers)
