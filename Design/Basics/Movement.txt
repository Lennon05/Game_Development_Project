Movement:
General movement for the game should feel weighty, your a survivor, not an Italian plumber

Walking:
The most basic form of movement. I do not feel the need to explain this one. Will be limited to left and right.

Running:
Walking, but faster. Activated with shift.
Will be significantly louder than walking, and should be reserved for when the player is spotted.

Crouching:
Walking, but slower and more sneaky. Activated with either C or CTRL.
will lower the players hitbox and make enemies take longer to detect the player.
will not save players who have been spotted, they should try running.

Crawling:
Basically walking again, will only be utilized in specific ventilation sections. Not activated with a button press.

Jumping:
Classic jumping. will be activated with Space Bar.
Won't be a very tall jump, but enough to get over some gaps. No, players will not be able to jump over enemies (except maybe the crawler, but I'm undecided on that).

Mantling:
There will be desks, crates, and other low objects that block players on their linear path.
Mantling will be activated with Space Bar, and will allow the player to climb over low objects

Doors:
The game will be split into a vast array of rooms and hallways, which will be accessed through doors
Players will be able to interact with E on doors either in the background or on the edges of a stage to transport to the next one
All enemies except The Mass will be stopped by doors.

Vents:
Basically reskinned doors, but lead to ventilation shafts players crawl through.

Ladders:
Some walls in the stage will have ladders against them.
Players will be able to climb said ladders by either jumping into them, or pressing E
