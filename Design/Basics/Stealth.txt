Stealth:
The player will not be immediately noticed (in most cases) when they enter a room
Players can attempt to use well timed movements and the environment, to remain undetected
If the player avoids combat, and remains out of sight, enemies will leave the player alone
Once a player is detected, the only way to lose detection would be to enter a different room or to kill all enemies in the current room

Detection Speed:
Players will have a default detection speed, which will change depending on what the player is doing and where they are relative to the enemy
If the player crouch walks and keeps their distance, they will be detected slowly
If the player is running near an enemy, they will be detected by enemies very quickly
Players will be able to take cover and block line of sight, which will prevent enemies from detecting them entirely
This system will be ignored once the player starts shooting loud weapons, then all enemies will know where the player is

Hiding Places:
Lockers, under desks, opened crates, etc.
These can be entered by the player by pressing E, and will stop enemies from detecting them entirely
this will not save or protect players if they have already been detected

Line of Sight:
Enemies will have a set of invisible rays coming from the front of their hit box, from top to bottom.
If the player is within this ray, the enemy will start detecting them
If the ray hits an object, the ray stops there
this basically means the enemies see in front of them
