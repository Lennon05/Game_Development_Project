Description:
A more heavily mutated form of the Husk, these creatures use what remains of their arms to crawl on the ground, or in some cases across ceilings
They are usually dormant until they detect prey, in which case they will fling themselves towards it, slashing and gnawing.

Gameplay:
Crawlers are to serve two main purposes:
1. make players have to switch their aim from high to low, so players don't just aim at the same level constantly
2. jumpscares. Lots of jumpscares

If a crawler is on a ceiling, it will jump down after the player, skipping the usual detection system
Otherwise, they follow normal detection rules

Crawlers are faster than a Husk, but take less damage to kill.
In practice, their faster speed should make them priority targets for players, as they will run faster than a player can.
