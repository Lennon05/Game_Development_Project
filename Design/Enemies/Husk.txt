Description:
What remains of the crew. Mutilated, bloodied, a husk of what was once there
They patrol the halls and kill anything in their way

Gameplay:
The basic enemy. They search for the player, they chase the player, they (try to) kill the player.
They are the most common across the game, and will probably be the enemy players kill the most

Basically your standard zombie. move slowly when they have not detected the player, and run just slightly slower than the player once they have been alerted.
