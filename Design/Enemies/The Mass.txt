Description:
A hulking mass of flesh. Unstoppable.
If it finds you, you're probably already dead.
You're only hope is to run.

Gameplay:
A large, imposing enemy. One the player distinctly does not want to take on
can take serious punishment, and can't be killed, only temporarily stopped.

The Mass will be triggered by specific actions related to progression, before either chasing the player or standing in their way
This serves to either create intense set pieces, or to more overtly encourage stealth in some sections, else the player will have to waste mass amounts of ammo.
