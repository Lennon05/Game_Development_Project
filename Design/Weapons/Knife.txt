Description:
A utility knife, issued to one of the crew members.
not much, but better than nothing.

Gameplay:
The "oh no, I've run out of ammo" weapon.
allows the player to slash a short distance ahead of them.
weak damage, and incredibly risky to use.

The knife is the first weapon a player will acquire, and will be unique in the fact it takes up no inventory space.
can be used quietly, but its not strong enough to kill any enemies in one swing, so stealth still won't last too long.
purposefully terrible, to discourage its use. But it will be there, as an option.
