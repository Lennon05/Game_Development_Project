Description:
A revolver issued to ship captains. It's chambered in .44 magnum with a 6 round cylinder.

Gameplay:
For lack of a better term, the "F*** YOU" weapon.

A late game reward for players who explore. 
Does incredibly high damage, destroying almost anything in 1 shot.
Will penetrate similar to the shotgun, but through less targets without losing damage.

Ammo is very scarce, any spare boxes only coming with 1 round

Powerful, but should be used wisely
