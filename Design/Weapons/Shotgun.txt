Description:
A pump-action shotgun, issued to security personnel for riot situations. holds 6 12 Gauge shells in its tube magazine.

Gameplay:
A stronger weapon than the handgun, but with significantly less ammo.
Perfect for large hordes.

While I'm uncertain if I'll be adding a whole spread mechanic (as that sounds hard to program) the shotgun will still be able to shoot through multiple targets.
this will mean that players can stun hordes that are chasing, and use this opportunity to run or to kill many of the horde.
Damage will decrease as shots penetrate targets, but stun times will not.

this gives the shotgun some purpose outside of horde clear
high damage on single targets, high pen stun for large groups.
